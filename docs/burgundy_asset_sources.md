# Burgundy + Chocolate | Style asset catalogue and motion direction

Updated 2026-11-22. Based on user-provided Pinterest screenshots IMG_3323–3327, plus two earlier short screen recordings. These are mood references, NOT licensed source material.

## Visual design contract

Palette:
- Oxblood #4B1B2E; deep burgundy #681B32; dark wine #350E1D; chocolate #4F3329
- Ivory #F5E8DE; muted rose #C998A3; optional champagne highlight #C7A77E
- Dark feminine editorial: dramatic and tactile rather than bright pink, neon or film damage.
- Human speaker remains fullscreen, natural skin tones; burgundy only as graded shadows and decorative layers.
- Typography: elegant serif titles with small light sans subtitles, lightly outlined/shadowed, composed as phrases rather than popping single words. Preserve all manual font / layout adjustments.

## Motifs from screenshots

- Satin bow, translucent organza bow
- Burgundy lily/rose, pressed petals, cherries
- Wax seal and embossed heart
- Red lace heart and ribbon
- Painted burgundy heart / hand drawn arrow
- Red silk folds, wine-red leather grain, alternating cream-burgundy stripe
- Perfume, letter/envelope, journal, books, pearl/champagne highlights
- Dark luxe still-life with coffee, chocolate and red flowers
- Glossy chrome ornament only sparingly
- Decorative collage should avoid brand logos and commercial product packaging unless uploader owns rights

## Montage behaviour

- On-camera dialogue is primary. No unconditional full-frame overlays.
- Animated prop overlay at exact matched words, only if semantics are clear. Decorative keyword set: heart/love -> lace/wax heart; flower -> burgundy rose/lily; present/ribbon -> bow; letter/history -> wax sealed envelope; fashion/luxury/style -> satin; coffee -> cup; books/learning -> journal; time -> watch.
- Micro transitions: 220–480 ms silk reveal, slow ribbon swipe, light paper fold, wax stamp; no VHS scratches/projector effects (they belong to Vintage).
- Fullscreen cutaway only by deliberate semantically anchored speech passage, ~1.3–2 seconds, preserve speech.
- Use SFX soft textile rustle with satin, ribbon swish with bow, wax stamp with seal, paper leaf with collage, light glass clink with beauty objects; no projector/film shutter unless user explicitly combines styles.
- Maintain user controls insert density and volume; avoid collision with subtitles or face.
- Uploaded image screenshot is a research example, never reused as sticker sheet; do not bundle branded commercial items or Pinterest screenshots into distributed code.
